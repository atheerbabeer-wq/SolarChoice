WordsShort definitions.
MW, MWh

Power and energy. 1 MW for 1 hour is 1 MWh.

W/m²

Sunlight strength. Full sun is about 1,000.

GHI, DNI

All light, and direct beam only. Mirrors use DNI.

DC, AC

Panels make DC. The factory uses AC.

LV, MV

Low voltage under 1 kV. Medium 1 to 35 kV.

halala (hal)

100 halalas = 1 SAR. $1 = 3.75 SAR.

Cost per kWh

Yearly cost of the plant divided by the energy it delivers.

Payback

Build cost divided by yearly bill saving after running cost.

Capacity factor

Energy made as a share of running at full power all year.

Stow

Turning mirrors away in high wind.

Soiling

Dirt on glass.
